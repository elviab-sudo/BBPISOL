Training

BBPI Solutions offers Instructional Design and Curriculum Development in collaboration with organizations to supplement their LMS/programs with unique professional in- house offerings needed to establish a qualified and skilled work force.

From one-on-one to instructor-led, our team of certified trainers deliver innovative trainings that can be customized to meet your needs while also leveraging common technology mediums (e.g. e-Learnings, Virtual Learning Labs and webinars) to accommodate the varied individual learning styles.

Our courses range from all levels of Microsoft Office and SharePoint to management trainings such as Effective Communications for Managers, Requirements Management, Risk Mitigation, Leadership Development, Customer Service and Organizational Skills.
